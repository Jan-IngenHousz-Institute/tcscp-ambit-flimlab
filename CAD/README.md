# CAD: light-tight measuring jig

A cup that sits on the leaf and keeps room light out. It aims three things at one spot:

- Detection comes in from −X at 45°, through a FiberPort PAF2-7B with a Ø25 mm emission filter and the multimode fibre to the SPAD.
- Excitation comes in from −Y at 45°, from a bare SMA905 fibre in a printed insert. There is no collimator, so the beam spreads over the leaf.
- The AMBIT sits on top and looks down through a window, with the lowest point of its casing 30 mm above the leaf.

The detection plate is the filter-holder Body in `FLIMLab-meas-setup.FCStd`. The generator places it at the port and fuses it into the cup, so editing that Body changes the cup on the next run.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `FLIMLab-meas-setup.FCStd` | The filter-holder Body, hidden and placed at the port, and the group "jig (generated by jig_generator.py)" |
| `jig_generator.py` | Builds every jig part from the PARAMS at the top of the file. Not in the repository since commit `6ad4419`, see Regenerate. |
| `export/jig-cup.stl`, `jig-lid.stl`, `jig-sma-insert.stl` | Print files |
| `export/jig-parts.step` | Cup, lid and insert as solids, for other CAD tools |
| `export/preview-assembly.png`, `preview-sections.png` | Quick look at the assembly and at sections through the spot |
| `fiber-port-filter-holder.FCStd` | The older, original stand-alone holder with Ø2.5 holes |
| `FLIMLab-meas-setup.before-jig.FCStd` | The file as it was before the generator first ran. Also removed in `6ad4419`. |

The generated group also holds reference shapes. The AMBIT casing and the insert are positioned copies. The FiberPort, the filter, the SMA connector, the detection view, the excitation cone and the leaf are envelopes. The FiberPort and SMA envelopes are approximations, not the vendor's geometry.

## Regenerate

Commit `6ad4419` removed `jig_generator.py` and `FLIMLab-meas-setup.before-jig.FCStd` from the repository. To regenerate, bring back their last committed versions first with `git checkout 17b8198 -- CAD/jig_generator.py CAD/FLIMLab-meas-setup.before-jig.FCStd`.

Close the file in FreeCAD first, then run from the repository root:

```
"%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\FreeCAD 1.1\bin\freecadcmd.exe" CAD\jig_generator.py
```

- The script needs the AMBIT casing STEP `A280.0101.000_3d_JII Asens._Assembly_Rev06.STEP` in Downloads. Set `AMBIT_STEP` to use another path.
- The first run slices the casing, which took 3 to 8 minutes here. The script caches the result in `CAD/.jig_cache/`, and later runs take about 2 minutes.
- Each run replaces the generated group, saves the FCStd and rewrites `export/`. The view settings of the previous version are kept.
- Running it as a macro inside FreeCAD, through Macro > Macros... > Execute, also colours the parts and leaves the file open. Nobody has tested that path yet.
- The script prints PASS or FAIL for each of these checks:
  - Valid solids.
  - AMBIT clear of cup and lid.
  - Detection view and excitation cone unobstructed.
  - FiberPort and SMA envelopes clear.
  - Insert sliding over its whole travel.
  - Wall around the heat-set holes.
  - A sealed cavity once the FiberPort, insert, AMBIT and leaf are in place.
  - Closed, manifold STL meshes without self-intersections.

## Key numbers

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Opening on the leaf | 44 × 44 mm, flange 62 × 62 mm |
| FiberPort mating face | 38 mm from the spot along the 45° axis |
| Fibre tip distance | 36.7 to 48.2 mm, set by sliding the insert |
| Excitation spot, 50 µm 0.22 NA fibre | about 17 × 25 mm at 37 mm, 22 × 32 mm at 48 mm |
| AMBIT | lowest point 30 mm above the leaf, optics centred over the spot |
| Clearance around the AMBIT | 0.3 mm sideways, 0.1 mm below, 0.2 mm above |

The spot sizes assume the fibre output fills its NA. Light launched from the laser's single-mode output may underfill it and give a smaller spot.

## Parts to buy

- FC/PC to SMA905 patch cable, multimode Ø50 µm, 0.22 NA.
- 5 × M3 heat-set inserts, M3 × 5.7 mm for a Ø4.0 hole. 4 go in the cradle for the lid and 1 in the boss on the excitation tube.
- 4 × M3 × 12 screws for the lid, and 1 × M3 × 8 grub screw for the insert.
- 4 FiberPort screws through the Ø3.0 plate holes, about 12 mm long to pass the 8 mm plate and engage. Check the thread on the PAF2-7B drawing.
- Black foam tape, 6 mm wide and 2 to 3 mm thick, about 25 cm for the flange, plus a scrap to seal where the AMBIT tail leaves the lid.
- Ø25 mm emission filter, at most 3.0 mm thick, which is the pocket depth in the holder Body.

## Printing

Use black PETG or PLA with 3 or more perimeters. Some black filaments pass near-infrared light, so test before trusting it.

- **Cup.** Print it with the flange on the bed and no supports. The inner ceiling bridges about 11 mm. The short opening cone at the excitation stop overhangs about 60° over 2 mm.
- **Lid.** Print it with the flat top on the bed.
- **Insert.** Print it standing on its inner face, stud up.

## Assembly

1. Melt the heat-set inserts in, four in the cradle top and one in the side boss of the excitation tube.
2. Drop the filter into the pocket, put the FiberPort on the plate, and screw it from inside the cup. The screw heads go up the four Ø6.5 tunnels through the open bottom.
3. Screw the SMA connector onto the insert stud. The coupling nut cuts its own thread the first time. If it will not start, reduce `stud_d` to 6.0 and reprint.
4. Slide the insert into the tube, set the tip distance, and lock it with the grub screw.
5. Lay the AMBIT in the cradle, leaf side down, with the head toward the excitation port and the cable out of the +Y tongue. Close the lid with the four screws.
6. Put foam tape on the flange and place the cup on the leaf.
7. Check that the cup is light-tight. With the laser off, compare SPAD count rates with the room lights on and off.

## Things to measure or check

- **Ferrule length.** `ferrule_len` is 9.8 mm, measured from the SMA connector shoulder. Measure yours. A different length only shifts the tip distance.
- **AMBIT signal.** At 30 mm the AMBIT LEDs light the leaf roughly 50 times weaker than the clothespin clip does at 3 to 4 mm. The 250 mA flash may no longer saturate, so check the AMBIT signal and the flash response before relying on it.
- **Filter thickness.** A filter thicker than 3.0 mm stops the FiberPort sitting flat. Deepen the pocket in the holder Body, save, close and rerun.
